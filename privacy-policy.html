Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Cortvista provides 9th Math Solutions — Punjab Board, an independent English-medium study reader. This policy describes information handled by the app, optional Google services and external links. No account, sign-in or cloud study sync is required.

2. Study information stored on your device

The app stores bookmarked PDF pages, last-opened chapters, reading positions, pages visited, study days, search history when used, text size and reminder preferences on your device. It may also store extra offline PDF copies and cached app configuration. This information makes bookmarking, reading progress and resume work. Cortvista does not upload your chapter choices, bookmarks, reading history or search terms to a study server.

3. Network connections and app updates

The included chapter PDFs work offline. The app checks whether a network connection is available to show an offline status. When Firebase is configured, Remote Config retrieves announcements, store-update information, support/policy links, advertising switches and reader feature settings. It uses a Firebase installation identifier, and network requests expose connection information such as an IP address to the service. Checks are attempted when you open or resume the app after the configured interval of three to five days, normally four. The app does not poll while closed, silently replace the notes, or enable Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics for study tracking.

4. Optional advertising and consent

When advertising is configured and enabled, Google AdMob and its consent service may process IP addresses, device or installation identifiers, app and device information, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect advertising. The app requests non-personalized ads with teen age treatment and a general-audience content rating. Non-personalized does not mean that no data is processed. Ads appear only in the Home banner; they are not placed over solutions. Consent or privacy choices are shown where required by the service. If available, reopen them in Profile → Ad privacy choices. Google’s policies explain its processing and retention.

5. Reminders and device permissions

Study reminders are optional local notifications scheduled at 6 PM in your device’s time zone. Enabling them may request notification permission. You can turn them off in Reading preferences or device settings. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location for studying. Saving an extra PDF copy uses the app’s local storage.

6. Sharing PDFs and external websites

Sharing a chapter opens your device’s share menu. A PDF and its source/licence reference are passed to the destination you choose. That destination controls what happens next. Opening source, licence, privacy or app-store links connects to external services with their own terms and privacy policies. Cortvista does not control those services or use this policy to change the rights attached to a shared PDF.

7. Retention, deletion and your controls

Saved study information and extra PDF copies remain on your device until removed. Profile → Reading preferences → Clear saved study data deletes bookmarks, reading history, search history, study-day records and extra PDF copies. It preserves the included notes and reading/reminder preferences. Removing a bookmark deletes that bookmark. Device settings can revoke notification permission or clear all app data; uninstalling generally removes app-local data. Operating-system backups may retain or restore app data according to your backup settings. Clearing local study data does not erase information already processed by Google or a share destination; their privacy controls and retention rules apply.

8. Students and younger users

The app is designed for secondary-school students, generally ages 13–17. It does not ask students to enter a name, email, phone number or date of birth to study. Parents or guardians can help manage device permissions, backups and ad privacy choices. Do not send sensitive personal information in support messages. Any advertising remains subject to the service’s age and consent settings.

9. Security and international processing

Study records are stored in the app’s device storage, without a separate Cortvista cloud database. Protection also depends on your device lock, operating system and backup settings; no system can guarantee complete security. When enabled, Google services may process information outside your country under their own published privacy and security arrangements.

10. Privacy requests and changes

Contact Cortvista for questions about this policy or a privacy request. Explain your request without including passwords or unnecessary student details. Requests concerning Google or another destination may also need to be directed to that service. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location and the relevant processing. If the app’s practices change, Cortvista will update this policy and its effective date; material changes will be communicated through the app or its store listing.
