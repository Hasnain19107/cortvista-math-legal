Data Controls and Deletion

Effective 9 October 2026

No Cortvista account is created

9th Math Solutions — Punjab Board does not provide account registration or a Cortvista cloud study profile. There is therefore no Cortvista account to delete.

Delete saved study information

Open Profile → Reading preferences → Clear saved study data and confirm. This removes this device’s bookmarks, reading history, searches, study-day records and extra PDF copies. The 13 included chapter PDFs and reading/reminder preferences remain available.

Remove all local app data

You can clear app storage through Android Settings or uninstall the app. Device backups may retain or restore data according to your backup settings. Turn reminders off in Reading preferences or device settings.

Optional third-party services

When configured, Firebase handles installation/configuration requests and Google AdMob handles advertising/consent data. Clearing local study data does not erase data already processed by those providers. See the Privacy Policy and provider privacy controls for their retention and deletion practices.

Contact Cortvista

For a privacy request, email the developer contact below. Describe what you want deleted without sending passwords, identity documents or unnecessary student information. Cortvista cannot access local bookmarks or reading records unless you separately choose to share them with support.
