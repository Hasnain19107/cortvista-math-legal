Help and Support

Effective 9 October 2026

Contact the developer

Cortvista provides 9th Math Solutions — Punjab Board as independent study support. For help, corrections or rights enquiries, email the contact below. Include your app version, chapter/exercise/page and a short explanation. Do not send passwords or private student details.

Offline reading

All 13 English chapter PDFs are included in the app. Choose a chapter and an exercise. Use page navigation, pinch zoom, bookmarks and PDF text search where available. Some mathematical labels are images; exercise shortcuts work without text search.

Updates and configuration

Configured versions check Firebase Remote Config on app startup or resume when the three-to-five-day interval is due. The usual interval is four days. Store updates replace app content only after you install an updated release. Announcements do not replace textbook questions.

Fixing a reading problem

Restart the app and try the chapter again. Check free device storage before saving extra copies. Use Profile → Reading preferences for text size, reminders and local data controls. Keep your current official textbook available when checking a solution.
